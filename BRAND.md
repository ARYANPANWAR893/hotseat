# Hotseat brand kit

**Idea:** the interview you rehearse out loud, so the real one feels familiar. Name = the "hot seat" every candidate dreads.
**Tagline:** Sit in the hotseat before the real one.
**Voice:** first person, warm, direct. The interviewer talks to you ("Hi Rahul, ready for round 4?"). No "AI-powered", no exclamation marks.
**Hashtags:** #Hotseat #GnaniAI

## Type
Headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** 700/800, tight tracking. Body/UI: **Figtree**. No serif, no mono labels.

## Shape and character
Big radii (24-32px), flat fills, no outlines or shadows. The three interviewers are illustrated characters (Priya, Arjun, Mr. Kapoor) and are the brand's face: they appear on the dashboard hero, onboarding questions, interviewer picker, the interview call and the scorecard. The interview is a video call: interviewer large, you in a small self-view, round controls.

## Mark
Lime speech bubble with three voice bars, on forest. Wordmark: Hotseat, Bricolage 800.
